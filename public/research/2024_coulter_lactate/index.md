This project evaluates a noninvasive wearable optical sensor for continuous lactate monitoring during cardiac surgery and critical care.

### Research Focus
- Evaluate continuous wearable lactate sensing in a clinical setting
- Compare optical sensor trends with standard clinical measurements
- Advance the technology toward real-time monitoring of perfusion and sepsis risk

### Funding
- **Wallace H. Coulter Foundation Translational Research Partnership**
