This project advances **SpO₂ Lumos**, a wearable optical sensing platform for continuous oxygen-saturation monitoring.

### Research Focus
- Improve signal quality across finger- and wrist-worn configurations
- Evaluate optical materials and refine the wearable hardware
- Advance device testing, analysis, and regulatory planning

### Funding
- **NIH Research Evaluation and Commercialization Hub (REACH)**
- **Virginia Innovation Partnership Corporation (VIPC)**
