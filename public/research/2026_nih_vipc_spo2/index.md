This project advances a multi-wavelength wearable pulse oximeter designed to improve continuous oxygen-saturation monitoring across diverse skin tones.

### Research Focus
- Improve oxygen-saturation measurement consistency across diverse skin tones
- Refine finger- and wrist-worn sensing hardware and signal-correction methods
- Advance device validation, commercialization, and regulatory planning

- **NIH Mid-South Research Evaluation and Commercialization Hub (REACH)**
