This project develops an ultraviolet filtration approach intended to reduce the risk of bloodstream infections by reducing microbial contamination in intravenous fluids.

### Research Focus
- Develop a medical-device approach that uses ultraviolet light for infection prevention
- Evaluate the system's ability to reduce microbial contamination
- Advance the technology toward clinical translation

### Funding
- **NIH Research Evaluation and Commercialization Hub (REACH)**
- **Virginia Innovation Partnership Corporation Commonwealth Commercialization Fund**
