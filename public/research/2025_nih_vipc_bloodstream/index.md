This project develops an ultraviolet filtration approach intended to reduce microbial contamination in intravenous fluids and lower the risk of bloodstream infections.

### Research Focus
- Develop and refine a compact ultraviolet filtration device
- Evaluate filtration performance and device integration
- Advance the technology toward preclinical and clinical translation

### Funding
- **NIH Research Evaluation and Commercialization Hub (REACH)**
- **Virginia Innovation Partnership Corporation Commonwealth Commercialization Fund**
