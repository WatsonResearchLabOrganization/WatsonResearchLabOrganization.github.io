Current pulse oximeters can exhibit higher error under hypoxic conditions when skin-tone variability is not adequately represented during device development and validation. This paper introduces a wearable device that combines multispectral spectroscopy with pulse-oximetry sensing, enabling paired measurements for studying skin-tone-dependent error in both wrist- and fingertip-worn form factors.
